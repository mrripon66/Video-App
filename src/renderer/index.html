A free allowance, not unlimited generation

Hugging Face currently lists about 2 minutes of GPU time per day for anonymous visitors, and 5 minutes for a free account. This app uses anonymous access; it does not connect an account. Limits, shared queues, and Space availability can change.

GPU time is the provider’s compute allowance, not the length of your video. A batch of 500 prompts may take substantially longer than a few days. Automatic batches wait for confirmed quota refusals.

Opening the Space in a browser lets you check its current status. A browser login does not sign this desktop app in.

Voice prompts

Voice is transcribed locally with Windows speech or the included offline Whisper model. No provider credits or speech language download is needed.

Always in your hands.

An automatic batch resumes when you reopen the studio. Pause ends its schedule.

  • Click Generate to start your prompt directly.
  • Only free providers are enabled. There is no automatic purchase or paid overage.
  • Pausing stops new submissions. A request already sent may still use free GPU quota.
  • Your queue and downloaded videos are saved on this PC. Submitted prompts are sent to the selected Hugging Face Space.
